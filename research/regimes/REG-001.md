# REG-001 — High-volatility liquid session

## Draft causal definition

Classify the state using rolling, lagged volatility plus contemporaneous spread and depth percentiles calculated from history available through time `t`. Thresholds and reference populations must be frozen before evaluation.

## Warning

Do not define a regime by the subsequent outcome or tune it until the target effect appears. High volatility and high liquidity are separate dimensions; this combined node is illustrative and may need decomposition.
