# IND-002 — Delta Candle Pressure Divergence v3 port

> Research indicator only. Its usefulness in the user's prior workflow is an observation, not evidence of predictive or economic edge.

## Source and purpose

The source is the user's working MMT v2 script at `C:/Users/morde/OneDrive/Máy tính/mmt/v2/Delta Candle Pressure Divergence.js`. The current v3 port is `C:/Users/morde/OneDrive/Máy tính/agents/Edgelab/indicators/mmt/Delta Candle Pressure Divergence v3.mmt`.

The indicator describes bar-level aggressive-flow pressure, segments persistent pressure into impulses, retains selected impulse or neutral-pivot extremes as levels until touch, displays UTC-session CVD, and marks a strict price-versus-delta divergence. It is not a liquidation detector and does not by itself establish a reversal edge.

## How the v2 indicator works

1. Per-bar delta is aggressive buy volume minus aggressive sell volume. A rolling sum over `deltaPeriod` forms the pressure series.
2. The adaptation baseline excludes the current bar. The 92nd percentile sets color scale. Otsu's between-class threshold, constrained between the 68th and 88th percentiles, sets pressure entry. The exit threshold uses the larger of the 45th percentile and a fraction of entry.
3. Scale and thresholds rise faster than they fall. A three-state hysteresis machine emits bullish, neutral, or bearish pressure, avoiding rapid color flipping near one threshold.
4. The rolling delta divided by adaptive scale drives a five-stop color gradient, with intensity and gamma shaping.
5. A completed run of non-neutral state becomes an impulse. If it is long and intense enough, its price extreme may become a horizontal level that extends until first touch.
6. CVD resets at each UTC day and may be shown raw or normalized to that session's running absolute peak. Volume is normalized behind it.
7. Divergence compares a completed same-direction impulse with recent same-direction impulses. Price must make a new extreme while both normalized peak delta and normalized mean delta weaken, and ending delta must wane versus the impulse's earlier peak.

## Re-evaluation

### Strong design choices

- It measures flow directly from buy and sell volume rather than inferring pressure from candle direction.
- The current bar is excluded from the v2 adaptive baseline, reducing mechanical self-normalization.
- Entry/exit hysteresis and asymmetric threshold smoothing are appropriate for a state display.
- Divergence requires peak, mean, and waning confirmation, which is substantially stricter than a two-point price/delta comparison.
- Divergence is evaluated before the current impulse is recorded, preventing self-comparison.

### Material weaknesses

- The v2 live `potentialExtreme` path can emit and permanently deduplicate a divergence during a forming bar. If the bar retreats, the marker can disagree with a later reload. Historical evaluation instead waits for a state change, so live/backfill parity is not guaranteed.
- `peakDelta` in the live path includes the forming bar for the climax gate, while `peakScaled`, `meanScaled`, and waning use only closed bars. The same candidate is therefore qualified with two different information sets.
- The freeze-on-first-write guard prevents closed-bar recoloring, but it also discards legitimate late corrections. Stability is gained by accepting stale values rather than by defining a settled-data boundary.
- The v2 adaptive calculation sorts the lookback twice on every update, including forming ticks. With long custom windows this is unnecessary event-loop work.
- Inactive levels remain in an ever-growing JavaScript array, so touch checking becomes progressively more expensive during long sessions.
- The divergence baseline independently takes the maximum peak and maximum mean across recent legs. Those maxima can come from different impulses, creating a synthetic comparison leg.
- Raw delta divergence remains contemporaneous descriptive evidence. It has not been shown to predict an executable reversal after controls and costs, consistent with `REJ-001`.

## v3 port decisions

- Pressure candles remain live/forming; impulse completion, divergence markers, levels, and alerts are close-confirmed.
- The forming candle explicitly uses `chart.forming` values. An optional developing-divergence preview is temporary, uses a stable entity key, never mutates impulse history, and never triggers an alert.
- The v2 percentile/Otsu logic is retained through a causal 64-bin histogram over prior closed observations. It is an approximation rather than bit-for-bit numeric parity, but reduces work from repeated full sorts to a bounded pass.
- The pressure engine does not become signal-ready until it has a complete rolling-delta window and a full prior-only adaptation baseline.
- Levels and entity pools are bounded by `maxRetainedLevels`; old slots are evicted deterministically.
- V3 confirmed/forming views replace manual freeze-on-first-write behavior. Late platform corrections are handled by normal v3 replay semantics rather than silently ignored.
- Same-direction impulses are kept in separate bounded rolling buffers. Divergence compares with the immediately preceding same-direction impulse so price, peak pressure, and mean pressure come from one coherent reference leg.
- Each impulse bar is normalized by the adaptive scale known at that bar. Peak, mean, ending pressure, and prior peak are therefore not retroactively divided by the scale at the impulse end.
- Waning pressure qualifies only when the price extreme occurred within `maxExtremeLagBars` of the impulse end.
- Confirmed divergence markers are placed at the confirmation bar and confirmation price. The earlier extreme remains explicit in the alert payload, preventing hindsight-backdated signal placement.
- The original presets, pressure gradient, hysteresis, UTC-session CVD, volume backdrop, impulse alerts, neutral pivot levels, and strict peak/mean/waning divergence are retained.

## Implemented correction — 2026-09-13

The missing v3 artifact was reconstructed from its prior recorded patches, then corrected in place. The v2 source remains unchanged. The corrected port removes the v2 hybrid forming/closed divergence calculation, the synthetic strongest-of-N comparison leg, ending-scale normalization, unbounded level retention, and historical marker backdating.

## Visual presentation — 2026-09-13

The user's supplied images were treated strictly as visual references. The default `Blue / Red` palette uses deep red and blue midtones with bright extrema on dark charts; `Aurora`, `Muted Pro`, and fully custom palettes are also available. Pressure candle and ribbon opacity follows a nonlinear magnitude curve. A slim zero-centered pressure histogram exposes the same normalized pressure already used for candle coloring. Its colors saturate at magnitude 100, but its height no longer hard-clamps there: values above 100 follow a continuous rational soft knee toward a configurable 200 default asymptote, with a separate 240 default pane range. This preserves ordering among strong bars and avoids the repeated flat columns caused by the prior hard cap. Session CVD is rendered as a crisp magnitude-graded line above a translucent wide under-stroke, with the volume backdrop disabled by default. Confirmed and developing divergence markers use bounded glow/core pairs. The neutral-visibility input uses a new persistent key with a 150-255 range and default 233 to avoid retaining the earlier 120 ceiling.

Normalized session CVD no longer divides by a denominator that already includes the current bar, which forced every incremental session extreme to exactly `+/-100`. It now divides by the prior confirmed session peak, preserves `100` as that causal reference, permits new extremes to expand toward a configurable `+/-200` default cap, and automatically keeps at least `+/-300` pane headroom or 25 units beyond a raised expansion cap. The first bar of a new UTC session still initializes at magnitude 100. This is a display-normalization change only and does not affect pressure, impulse, divergence, level, or alert logic; it does not strengthen the indicator's evidence or trading interpretation.

## Net-pressure and activity presentation — 2026-09-14

An experimental two-line view plotted rolling buy share and sell share on the same 0–100 scale. Because both used current gross volume as their denominator, they always summed to 100 and were exact reflections around 50. The second trace therefore added no independent information and was removed.

The replacement `Net + activity` mode keeps the actual signed, adaptive normalized delta as one zero-centered line. Blue/red position and color encode direction and pressure magnitude. A wide under-stroke varies only in opacity according to the percentile rank of current rolling gross aggressive volume (`buyVolume + sellVolume`) against a bounded baseline of earlier confirmed rolling-gross observations. Position and glow therefore encode separate quantities: directional net pressure and contemporaneous activity intensity. The forming bar is compared only with previously confirmed baseline observations; the current rolling-gross value is appended after the confirmed bar is rendered.

Positive and negative entry/exit guides are projected through the same sensitivity, gamma, and soft-knee transform as the pressure line. They expose the actual hysteresis boundaries in display coordinates rather than introducing visual-only thresholds. The mode waits for the existing pressure baseline and the new gross-activity baseline to fill. It changes no impulse, divergence, level, candle, or alert rule and remains an unvalidated research display.

## Automatic OHLCV/activity regime adaptation — 2026-09-14

The former fixed `Automatic` preset is now named `Balanced`. `Automatic` is an experimental causal adapter that uses data available on Free-compatible historical OHLCV bars: a 20-bar average range in basis points as a volatility proxy and rolling gross aggressive volume as an activity proxy. Each is percentile-ranked against 240 earlier confirmed observations. The current observation is appended only after the bar has been processed, so it cannot normalize or classify itself.

The detector emits five descriptive states: Balanced, Quiet, Active, Volatile, and Thin-risk proxy. The last state means unusually high OHLCV range with unusually low gross aggressive volume; without spread and depth it must not be interpreted as measured illiquidity. A different state must persist for four confirmed bars by default. Until both baselines are full, the adapter remains Balanced. The historical handler processes bars chronologically, so available chart history warms the same state machine later used at the live edge; it does not require interactive Bar Replay.

Only sensitivity, threshold adaptation speed, neutral-exit ratio, and minimum impulse climax change. Delta period and adaptation lookback remain fixed to preserve the underlying measurement contract. New target settings are smoothed by 25% per confirmed bar and affect only the next bar. The pressure pane can show the applied numeric state (`0` Balanced/warm-up, `1` Quiet, `2` Active, `3` Volatile, `4` Thin-risk proxy) so transitions can be audited.

All percentile cutoffs, persistence, and parameter maps are design defaults, not fitted or validated findings. The first discriminating comparison is `Automatic` versus fixed `Balanced` on identical history: regime occupancy and transition rate, pressure-state firing rate, impulse duration, divergence count, and forward-return distributions must be reported separately by state. Any apparent benefit must survive a fixed-rule baseline and executable-cost analysis before promotion.

## Impulse-gated big-trade bubbles — 2026-09-15

The overlay now defaults to a historical taker hybrid. A configurable MMT `data.vd` USD trade-size bucket supplies confirmed bar-level aggressive-flow delta. A bar qualifies only when the estimated absolute quote value of that bucket delta (`abs(bucket delta) * close`) exceeds the bubble threshold and the pressure state closes non-neutral. Its sign chooses blue/buy or red/sell. When the matching `data.volume` snapshot exists, the marker is anchored at the dominant same-side volume-profile price; otherwise it is placed at the candle close. The fallback is explicitly a bar location, not a reconstructed execution price.

Hybrid mode produces at most one aggregate net-flow bubble per confirmed bar. Its label begins with `~` because opposing large trades can cancel in volume delta and multiplying base-volume delta by the close is only an estimated quote notional. `data.volume` determines the price anchor but does not prove that the selected large-size trades executed at that level. MMT initially supplies at most 250 volume-profile snapshot periods, so longer `data.vd` history can retain bubbles with close-price fallback beyond the available profile window.

An `Individual prints` mode remains available. It subscribes to raw trades and buffers prints whose exact quote notional (`trade price * base size`) exceeds the threshold. A buffered print is rendered at its actual timestamp and price only after its candle closes in a non-neutral pressure state. Aggressive buys are blue and aggressive sells are red; both sides remain visible within an impulse because opposing large prints can be diagnostically relevant rather than treated as directional confirmation.

Bubble diameter grows sublinearly with notional and is capped. Optional centered labels show quote notional in thousands, matching the supplied visual reference (for example, `125` means approximately 125k in the quote currency). A coarse subscription floor is separately exposed in base units because MMT can filter `Trade.size` before delivery but cannot filter on computed quote notional. The per-candle buffer and chart entity pools are bounded; if more qualifying prints arrive than the configured per-candle limit, the newest are retained.

The appearance is user-editable without changing qualification: bubbles can follow the pressure palette or use independent buy/sell colors; body opacity, base size, maximum size, global scale, glow visibility/opacity/size, label color, and label size are exposed. Retained bubble count is configurable up to 2,000. Hybrid history defaults to 750 confirmed bars and exposes MMT bucket selection from 1 to 11. The raw-trade history request remains configurable from 1 to 168 hours and defaults to 24 hours.

This layer is contemporaneous visualization, not a new impulse rule or evidence that large prints predict continuation or reversal. Historical hybrid bubbles are available from confirmed aggregate sources but are not individual trades. In exact-print mode, `Trade.time` has one-second resolution, so multiple prints in the same second and near the same price can overlap. Exact historical bubbles still depend on plan-provided raw-trade history: MMT exposes historical raw trades on Ultra, while Free and Pro begin at the live edge. Increasing the requested hours cannot bypass that plan boundary.

2. Record spread and depth availability when MMT data permits it. Weak delta in a liquidity vacuum is not the same mechanism as passive absorption.

4. Compare immediately-prior-leg and causal median-baseline policies as separate ablations.
5. Compare confirmed markers with matched non-divergent impulses and executable forward returns before using alerts as entries.
