# IND-007 — Aurora Flow Momentum v2

> Research indicator only. This is an uncompiled successor to `IND-006`, not evidence of predictive or tradable edge and not Polish Quant's original code.

## Purpose

`IND-007` implements the improvement audit of the first Aurora Momo reconstruction while preserving that original as a separate file. The implementation is `indicators/mmt/aurora-flow-momentum-v2.mmt`.

The design remains a close-confirmed, panel-only composite. It separates momentum, signed aggressive pressure, relative activity, compression, and response decay rather than presenting them as independent confirmations.

## Material changes from IND-006

- **Correct participation gate:** activity below the configured threshold now suppresses the score. The gate ramps linearly from zero at the threshold to full weight at ordinary activity instead of imposing a minimum score-retention floor.
- **Stable composite scale:** the weighted momentum/flow mixture is causally normalized again before display. A smooth rational saturation maps it toward +/-20 without a hard clipping plateau.
- **Honest tape decomposition:** the prior unsigned tape line is renamed `Activity`. A distinct signed-pressure line uses rolling aggressor imbalance multiplied by capped relative activity.
- **Response-based exhaustion:** `EXTREME UP/DOWN` remains a magnitude state. `BUY/SELL RESPONSE DECAY` additionally requires aligned extreme score, elevated signed pressure, elevated activity, and unusually weak price response per unit pressure.
- **Improved compression state:** compression uses a z-score of causally smoothed, ATR-normalized bar range against its own longer baseline instead of a fixed close-deviation/ATR ratio.
- **Horizon transparency:** the three calculations are explicitly called 1x, 2x, and 4x lookbacks. Adaptive/global signing remains available, and a custom mode exposes the sign of every horizon.
- **Diagnostics:** an optional visual mode plots the three horizon components and normalized flow separately.
- **Missing activity:** a confirmed bar with zero gross aggressor volume reports `NO FLOW DATA` and does not enter a fabricated balanced-flow observation.
- **Visual scope:** Blue / Red is the default graded palette. Activity and signed-pressure lines have independent visibility, width, glow, and opacity controls. The script emits no main-chart plots or entities.

## Information-time contract

One `data.ohlcv` subscription and `on chart.close` handler drive every calculation. All score, activity, pressure, compression, and response-efficiency values are therefore close-confirmed. The word `Activity` is intentional: this build does not claim intrabar trade-tape responsiveness.

Rolling normalization is prior/through-t and causal. The additional composite and response-efficiency normalization lengthens warm-up but stabilizes interpretation across changing correlation regimes. No future pivots, centered filters, or backdated states are used.

## Remaining limitations

- The source formulas and fitted parameters remain unavailable; numerical parity with the author is unverified.
- Bar-level buy/sell volume depends on venue aggressor classification and cannot recover within-bar sequencing.
- Response efficiency is a bar proxy, not executable price impact. It lacks spread, depth, replenishment, queue position, and quote timestamps.
- The default 0.72 blend, one-day adaptive sign boundary, response thresholds, and compression threshold are research parameters, not fitted evidence.
- A response-decay state may precede pause, reversal, or renewed continuation. It must not be interpreted as a reversal signal without forward testing.
- Multiple rolling transforms and overlapping horizon returns can still create serial dependence and correlated observations.
