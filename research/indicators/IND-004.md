# IND-004 — Comet speed and density lab

> Prototype-unvalidated research indicator. It describes tape states and does not issue entries, exits, or reversal predictions.

## Purpose

Implement the measurement framework captured in `KI-20260913-004` without collapsing price velocity, actual trade intensity, and volume-per-move density into one opaque score. The indicator is designed to expose candidate active impulses and later response fade for replay and measurement under `OQ-002`.

## Artifacts

`indicators/mmt/comet-speed-density-lab.mmt`

`indicators/mmt/comet-speed-density-ribbon.mmt` is a compact visual variant. It preserves the same measurements and research-state thresholds but removes the card and markers. Its primary line plots signed joint speed/activity percentile, while the thinner companion line plots signed density percentile. Both inherit actual two-active-second price direction; no color flip is used to imply a reversal.

## Inputs and event clock

- MMT `data.trades` supplies individual trade price, size, aggressor side, and timestamps floored to Unix seconds.
- Trades are aggregated into active one-second buckets. A bucket is confirmed by the first trade arriving in a different second.
- Empty wall-clock seconds cannot be emitted by a trade-only handler and are not backfilled. The baseline therefore describes active seconds, not all elapsed seconds.
- Every component uses a causal two-active-second simple moving average, matching the supplied “2 period smoothing” with an explicit filter.
- Percentile ranks use only earlier completed samples; the current sample is appended after it is scored.
- `data.ohlcv` drives plotting of the most recently completed tape state. It is not used to manufacture trade-speed observations.

## Components

1. **Price speed:** the mean absolute tick displacement across the current and previous active-second buckets. A separately smoothed signed displacement supplies direction.
2. **Trade activity:** prior-baseline percentiles of smoothed trade count and gross traded size, combined transparently as 60% of the larger percentile plus 40% of the smaller.
3. **Density:** smoothed gross traded size divided by smoothed absolute tick displacement with a configurable denominator floor. It is displayed in quantity per tick and as a prior-baseline percentile.

Trade count, gross size, and price movement remain visible in the HUD. Density is not labeled as belief, absorption, or informed flow.

## Research states

- `NORMAL` (`0`): no special state or insufficient joint evidence.
- `THIN DRIVE` (`25`): price speed and activity exceed their percentile gates while density is unusually low; displacement is large relative to activity.
- `ACTIVE DRIVE` (`50`): speed and activity are high while density is neither extreme.
- `DENSE ACTIVITY` (`75`): volume per unit movement is unusually high. This can be absorption, churn, market making, or a denominator effect.
- `RESPONSE FADE` (`100`): after an active directional drive, activity remains high, density is high, and speed falls below a configurable fraction of the drive's causal peak.

Small chart markers identify the beginning of a directional drive. A larger opposite-color marker identifies the descriptive response-fade transition. Markers are placed at the first trade that makes the preceding second knowable; they are not backdated to a hindsight extreme.

## Intensity-ribbon presentation — 2026-09-13

The compact variant uses a zero-centered `Ice & Ember` scale: negative movement grades from deep crimson to bright red and positive movement grades from deep navy to electric blue. Each series has a translucent glow under-stroke and a crisp core whose opacity follows a nonlinear percentile-magnitude curve. `Aurora`, `Muted Pro`, and custom palettes are also available.

Ribbon height and color are display coordinates, not additional signal rules. The drive trace transparently combines speed and activity as 60% of the larger percentile plus 40% of the smaller; the density trace remains separate. The existing `THIN DRIVE`, `ACTIVE DRIVE`, `DENSE ACTIVITY`, and `RESPONSE FADE` definitions are unchanged.

## Warm-up, reset, and missing-data behavior

- Default baseline: 180 completed active seconds; default minimum warm-up: 45.
- A drive releases when activity falls below the configured release percentile. A directional flip starts a distinct drive unless the prior drive first meets the response-fade condition.
- The first bucket has no prior bucket and therefore uses its raw values; it still cannot qualify before warm-up.
- No zero is substituted for a missing trade bucket. Long gaps make the active-second interpretation stale until another trade arrives.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- This is a trade-price proxy, not executable bid/ask or microprice velocity.
- Percentile ranks adapt to the loaded venue, instrument, and recent active-second regime; they do not make raw quantity comparable across symbols.
- Two-bucket overlap induces serial dependence and can make clusters look more persistent.
- High density is mechanically sensitive to the movement floor and may reflect two-sided churn rather than absorption.
- Fast movement may already be over before the next-second confirmation can be acted upon.
- No spread, depth, fill, fee, latency, slippage, or market-impact model is included.
