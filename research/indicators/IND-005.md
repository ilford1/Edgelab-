# IND-005 — Book Replenishment and Reclaim

> Research indicator only. It describes a causal retreat-to-replenishment sequence and does not establish reversal expectancy or a trading strategy.

## Purpose

`IND-005` is an MMT Scripting v3 companion for `IND-002`, Delta Candle Pressure Divergence v3. Delta Pressure supplies the user's primary bar-level view of aggressive-flow pressure. This companion independently asks whether the book side being consumed begins to recover while price response to same-side flow decays, then waits for price to reclaim against the impulse.

The implementation is `indicators/mmt/book-replenishment-reclaim.mmt`. It does not read state from another indicator. Its local rolling buy-minus-sell volume is used only to select the consumed side and deduplicate one sequence per local flow impulse. The user should require directional agreement with Delta Pressure when evaluating the pair.

## Information-time contract

- A confirmed one-second `data.ohlcv` subscription drives all state transitions.
- A pull-only one-second `data.book` subscription is queried from the OHLCV close handler.
- If the newest book boundary is later than the confirmed OHLCV second, the script steps back one snapshot. It requires the selected book timestamp to equal the OHLCV timestamp; missing periods are unavailable rather than forward-filled.
- Activity, spread, near-touch quote value, and depth-distance percentile ranks use only earlier confirmed samples. The current observation is appended after the state decision.
- Candidate and reclaim markers are placed at their confirmation second and price. No marker is backdated to the earlier stress extreme.

## Measurements

1. **Flow context:** rolling buy minus sell volume over four confirmed seconds. Absolute imbalance divided by rolling gross volume supplies a direction gate, not an additional independent score.
2. **Activity:** current rolling gross flow ranked against 120 earlier rolling-gross observations.
3. **Spread stress:** best-ask minus best-bid in basis points, prior-only percentile-ranked.
4. **Near-touch retreat:** quote value on the consumed side inside the larger of two basis points or one tick. Low current depth relative to its prior distribution is expressed as a retreat percentile.
5. **Depth distance:** quantity-weighted average distance of displayed depth from the consumed-side best quote inside a fixed ten-basis-point band, prior-only percentile-ranked.
6. **Response efficiency:** same-direction midpoint progress in ticks divided by current same-side volume normalized to its prior mean. The raw response-retention percentage and its depth/spread denominators remain visible in the chart HUD.

For aggressive buying, asks are the consumed side and a later downside reclaim is the candidate reversal direction. Aggressive selling mirrors the calculation on bids.

## State machine

1. `WAITING`: baselines are warming, book data is unavailable, the local flow gate is inactive, or the last same-direction sequence remains consumed.
2. `DRIVE`: activity and flow dominance pass their gates. The script tracks directional displacement, midpoint response, and the event extreme.
3. `BOOK STRESS`: the event has moved at least two ticks and reached sufficient peak response while spread, consumed-side depth distance, or near-touch retreat is stressed.
4. `ABSORPTION CANDIDATE`: for two confirmed seconds, same-side flow persists, response efficiency is at most 50% of the event peak, near-touch quote value has recovered to at least 1.25 times the stress trough, depth distance has retained at most 80% of its stress peak, and spread has retained at most 85% of its stress peak or left the stress percentile.
5. `RECLAIM CONFIRMED`: midpoint moves two ticks against the impulse from the absorption anchor. One confirmed marker and, when enabled, one close-confirmed alert are emitted.
6. `INVALIDATED` behavior: post-candidate continuation beyond eight ticks, excessive missing book data, or timeout clears the sequence. A confirmed or invalidated direction remains blocked until the local flow impulse releases, preventing repeated markers from the same event.

All numbers above are research defaults, not fitted or validated thresholds.

## Visual and alert contract

- The separate state histogram and diagnostics pane are replaced by a chart-native Aurora presentation. A compact top-left HUD identifies the active state and reversal-watch direction, with activity, retreat, depth-distance, response, depth-recovery, spread-retention, and book-alignment diagnostics available in the same card.
- After the baselines are ready, six fixed-span, right-anchored price bands summarize displayed value on the consumed side from the best quote through the configured outer book band. Each band is normalized only to the largest band in the current aligned snapshot. Opacity and glow intensity describe the current liquidity concentration without turning the display into a volume-profile silhouette or a historical z-score.
- Every band uses a translucent bloom layer beneath a brighter core. Neutral blue-gray denotes `WAITING` or `DRIVE`, violet denotes `BOOK STRESS`, pale ice denotes the candidate transition, cyan denotes a bullish reversal watch, and pink-red denotes a bearish reversal watch. Color is presentation of the existing state machine, not an extra confirmation input.
- A pale double-layer diamond marks the confirmed absorption candidate. A larger cyan or red double-layer diamond marks the confirmed reclaim.
- The optional diagnostics pane exposes activity percentile, spread percentile, near-depth retreat percentile, depth-distance percentile, response retention, near-depth recovery, and spread retention.
- Missing or misaligned book data produces an explicit `BOOK UNAVAILABLE` state and no candidate or reclaim.
- Only `RECLAIM CONFIRMED` can trigger an alert. Alert payloads include direction, confirmation price, activity percentile, response retention, near-depth recovery, spread, and event age.

## Limitations and falsifiers

- `data.book` provides period snapshots rather than a full order-by-order book event stream. Apparent replenishment may be fleeting, spoofed, or already stale by the next snapshot.
- Built-in range summaries may see more depth than individually iterable levels, depending on the user's data plan. Book history is also availability- and plan-limited.
- The local flow direction is related to Delta Pressure's input and must not be counted as independent confirmation. The intended incremental evidence is the book recovery and response-to-flow transition.
- Exchange timestamps, receive latency, hidden liquidity, cross-venue price discovery, and executable queue position are not observed by this script.
- The proposed mechanism is weakened if the marker occurs after most of the reversal, if apparent replenishment cancels before interaction, if reasonable band widths reverse the result, or if executable outcomes do not survive costs.
