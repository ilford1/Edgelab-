# IND-003 — Flow Shock x Delta Pressure sequence

## Status

Prototype-unvalidated. The source has been checked locally against the MMT v3 language constraints available in the workspace, but it has not yet been compiled or replayed inside an authenticated MMT chart.

## Purpose

Reduce repeated Flow Shock markers by treating delta candle pressure as an event context, not as another simultaneous score component. The prototype permits one armed event per confirmed non-neutral pressure impulse and upgrades that event only when its later stages occur.

This is a research-state display, not evidence of a predictive or economically exploitable reversal edge.

## Artifact

`indicators/mmt/flow-shock-pressure-sequence.mmt`

## Causal sequence

1. `WAITING`: either no confirmed delta-pressure context exists or the current pressure impulse has already been consumed.
2. `ARMED`: a tape shock exceeds the activity, dominance, and composite-score gates in the same direction as the last confirmed delta-pressure state.
3. Repeated qualifying shocks within that same pressure impulse update the stored peak and price extreme but do not create another arm marker.
4. `PRESSURE DECAY`: a completed tape second falls below the release percentile and live rolling-delta strength falls below a configurable fraction of its post-arm peak.
5. `RECLAIM CONFIRMED`: price moves a configurable number of ticks against the impulse extreme after decay.
6. `INVALIDATED`: continuation exceeds the configured tick limit, the sequence times out, or live pressure flips before confirmation.

## Inputs and event clocks

- Confirmed pressure context updates on `chart.close` from rolling candle delta and a prior-only adaptive magnitude baseline.
- Tape shock, live pressure decay, invalidation, and reclaim update on `tape.trade`.
- Tape activity uses completed and current one-second buckets because MMT trade timestamps expose Unix seconds. The implementation cannot identify a true millisecond pause.
- Price thresholds are expressed in `context.tickSize` units.

## Display contract

- Small halo: the first aligned Flow Shock arms the current pressure impulse.
- Medium halo: tape activity and delta-pressure strength have both decayed.
- Large opposite-color halo: price has reclaimed the required ticks after decay.
- The chart card shows readiness, pressure direction, sequence state, flow activity, pressure strength, and reclaim progress.
- The lower panel plots flow score, activity percentile, normalized pressure, the decay gate, and the discrete sequence stage.

## What this combination does and does not solve

The pressure impulse acts as a deduplication key, so signal frequency should be materially lower than raw per-second Flow Shock firing by construction. Directional alignment can also remove shocks that conflict with the prevailing confirmed delta-pressure state.

It does not establish greater accuracy. Both components partly reuse aggressive trade flow, so their apparent agreement is not independent confirmation. The sequence needs event-level replay and ablation against Flow Shock alone, pressure alone, and a simple cooldown baseline.

## Promotion checks

- Compile successfully in MMT v3 and inspect any API diagnostics.
- Replay at least two liquid and two thinner perpetual markets across quiet and liquidation-heavy sessions.
- Count arm, decay, confirm, invalidation, and timeout rates per pressure impulse.
- Compare forward excursion and executable net outcomes for the combined sequence, each component alone, and matched random/cooldown controls.
- Verify stability across threshold neighborhoods rather than selecting a single best setting.
