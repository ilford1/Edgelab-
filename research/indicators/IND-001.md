# IND-001 — Flow-shock and response-decay monitor

> Design only. This is not a validated signal.

## Implemented first stage

`indicators/mmt/flow-shock-halo.mmt` implements an unvalidated live aggressive-flow shock detector for MMT Scripting v3. It combines three interpretable components:

- activity percentile from per-second trade count and gross delivered trade size;
- dominant-side share from buy versus sell aggressive size;
- consequence percentile from aligned price displacement in ticks.

The implementation uses the raw trade handler, a causal rolling active-second baseline, a warm-up state, a release/re-arm transition, a viewport-anchored component card, a normalized lower measurement panel, and layered halo markers. It does not label events as liquidations and does not issue entries.

MMT's current `Trade.time` is floored from stored millisecond timestamps to Unix seconds. The prototype therefore measures active one-second buckets; empty seconds cannot be emitted by a trade-only handler and are not silently backfilled.

## Inputs for the later response-decay stage

Real-time trade flow, executable or mid-price progress, causal scale estimates, and data-quality flags.

## Output

Display response-efficiency level, rate of change, confidence/availability, and the impulse direction. Keep raw components visible so a ratio artifact is diagnosable.

The implemented first-stage card displays activity, dominance, consequence, direction, score, warm-up/normal/elevated/shock state, and an explicit research-only label. It renders from the chart update handler so it remains present before trade-history warm-up. The lower panel plots the 0–100 shock score, activity percentile, dominance share, consequence percentile, and score gate. A halo is emitted only on entry into a newly armed shock state.

## Intensity-ribbon variant — 2026-09-13

`indicators/mmt/flow-shock-intensity-ribbon.mmt` preserves the same causal active-second baseline, warm-up, component weights, gates, and re-arm rule while replacing the card-and-halo presentation with a compact two-line ribbon. The blue line is the buy-side Flow Shock score and the red line is the sell-side score. For whichever side is dominant, its ribbon value is numerically identical to the original detector's score; the non-dominant score is shown only to make the competing side inspectable.

Both lines use a wide translucent under-stroke plus a crisp core. A causal 0–100 score controls a nonlinear opacity curve and a dark-to-bright color scale, so ordinary observations remain deep navy or crimson and prior-baseline extremes approach electric blue or red. The default `Ice & Ember` palette matches the supplied visual reference; `Aurora`, `Muted Pro`, and custom endpoints are available. The palette changes presentation only and does not alter qualification.

## Guardrails

No future pivots, centered smoothing, repainting, or silent backfill. Define warm-up, denominator floors, missing-data behavior, update cadence, and venue semantics. The display must not imply a trade until `HYP-001` passes validation and execution gates.

## Validation status

The original source was checked against the official Scripting v3 manifest, catalog signatures, language specification, and documented HUD, plot/pane, and trade-handler examples on 2026-09-10. The ribbon variant was checked against the refreshed manifest and current color-scale and line-plot signatures on 2026-09-13. Neither variant has yet been compiled or replayed inside the MMT platform, so runtime and visual parity remain unverified.
