# IND-006 — Aurora Momo Engine reconstruction

> Research indicator only. This is a source-inspired reconstruction, not Polish Quant's original code and not evidence of predictive or tradable edge.

## Purpose

`IND-006` implements the behaviors disclosed in the Polish Quant Momo Engine thread and the complete settings menu supplied by the user. The implementation is `indicators/mmt/aurora-momo-engine-replica.mmt`.

The source discloses the architecture and intended semantics, but not the formulas, fitted weights, squeeze rule, label cutoffs, or marker reset logic. Those undisclosed parts are implemented transparently and exposed where they could materially change the result.

## Information-time and computation contract

- One confirmed `data.ohlcv` subscription drives the indicator. The three momentum horizons are causal chart-timeframe lookbacks of `length`, `length * step`, and `length * step^2`. This avoids mixing unaligned subscription clocks and gives the horizon control a literal, inspectable meaning.
- Each horizon uses close-to-prior-close change divided by ATR and the square root of its lookback. The horizons receive weights `1`, `decay`, and `decay^2`.
- Adaptive signing follows the source teaching: an effective horizon below one day is signed as mean reversion, while one day and above is signed as trend. The user can instead force trend or mean reversion at every horizon.
- Bar aggressor flow is `(buyVolume - sellVolume) / (buyVolume + sellVolume)`, then exponentially smoothed.
- Momentum and flow are separately rolling z-scored over the normalization lookback. Their undisclosed blend is exposed as `Momentum share`, defaulting to 0.72, with the remainder assigned to order flow. Three standard deviations map to the displayed +/-20 range before gating.
- Tape strength is smoothed gross aggressor volume divided by its own 96-bar baseline. Tape flow is rolling signed aggressor volume divided by rolling gross volume.
- Volume supplies conviction only: low tape strength can damp the score, but activity cannot choose direction or amplify the score above full conviction.
- Compression is inferred as twice the rolling close standard deviation divided by ATR being at or below the tightness setting. During compression, the score is multiplied by `Compression dampen` and the display is dimmed.
- Every directional value and state transition uses confirmed bars. No forming value, future pivot, or backdated marker is used.

## Exhaustion display boundary

The supplied exhaustion threshold remains visible as symmetric guides and informs the pane title's stretched-state label. At the user's request, the current build has no turn-marker state machine and emits no price-chart entities. The author's exact arming and reset rules remain unknown.

## Aurora visual contract

- A compact lower pane combines a magnitude-graded histogram with bloom, glow, and core score layers.
- A white tape-strength line is scaled by `Tape line height`; its glow uses the palette accent.
- The indicator is panel-only: it does not recolor candles, draw markers or HUD entities, or shade the main price chart. Compression shading is confined to the lower pane.
- A dedicated `Visual` tab contains the presentation controls. Aurora Borealis is the default; Blue / Red, Ice & Ember, Electric Orchid, Polar Night, and a custom palette are available without changing the engine.
- Semantic tape labels are derived summaries of the same tape inputs and must not be counted as additional confirmation.

## Limitations and falsifiers

- The source code and research notebook were not available through the public community catalogue, so formula parity is unverified.
- The 0.72 momentum share, one-day sign boundary, squeeze ratio, and tape label thresholds are disclosed reconstruction choices, not claims about the author's hidden implementation.
- Aggressor classification, bar construction, venue differences, missing bars, and rolling-window instability can change the score.
- A visual turn marker does not establish reversal expectancy. The reconstruction is weakened if reasonable parameter neighborhoods reverse its behavior, if order flow adds no conditional information beyond price, or if close-confirmed states arrive after most of the move.
