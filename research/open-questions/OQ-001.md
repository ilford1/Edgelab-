# OQ-001 — Does response decay transfer across venues?

## Question

If `HYP-001` appears on one venue, does its incremental information persist on other venues after reconciling aggressor flags, timestamp quality, fee tier, tick size, depth, latency, and market-share differences?

## Resolution evidence

Use a frozen definition and chronological samples from multiple venues. Separate same-venue replication from cross-venue transfer and consolidated-market lead/lag. A null transfer result should become a finding, not be discarded as inconvenient.

Following the practitioner claim captured in `KI-20260913-003`, split the test into two questions: (1) does a leader venue predict the local venue's next executable price, and (2) does enough lag remain after observation, decision, order, fill, impact, and fee delays for a non-market-maker? A visible catch-up with no executable residual is price discovery, not an edge available to the tested participant.
