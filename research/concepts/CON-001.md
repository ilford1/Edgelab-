# CON-001 — Impulse exhaustion

## Definition

A state in which maintaining directional price progress requires increasing flow, time, or liquidity consumption. Exhaustion is not equivalent to reversal and must not be labeled solely from a future-known peak or trough.

## Measurement boundary

Research may use retrospective impulse labels for analysis, but any deployable state estimate must specify what was observable at time `t`. Candidate evidence includes declining response efficiency, failed continuation, replenishment, and changing spread/depth, each tested separately.

## Open risks

Scale choice, volatility normalization, event segmentation, and exchange mechanics can create apparent exhaustion without predictive information.
