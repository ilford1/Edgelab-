# REJ-NNN — Rejected idea

## Rejected claim

State the original claim fairly.

## Decision and evidence

Record why it was rejected, the decisive experiment/findings, uncertainty, and date.

## Scope

Explain whether the mechanism, measurement, predictive claim, or implementation failed. Preserve any narrower part that remains plausible.

## Reopening conditions

Specify genuinely new evidence or a corrected design that would justify reconsideration.

## Graph update

Keep links to the original hypothesis, experiments, findings, and any successor via `supersedes`. Do not delete the rejected history.
