# HYP-001 — Diminishing response predicts failed continuation

> Architecture example only. This is an unvalidated hypothesis, not a market finding.

## Claim

Conditional on a directionally active impulse known at time `t`, a material decline in price response per unit same-side aggressive flow predicts a lower distribution of executable continuation over horizon `h` than matched events without response decay.

## Null

After controlling for pre-event trend, realized volatility, spread, depth, impulse age/magnitude, time of day, and venue, response decay adds no out-of-sample information about continuation.

## Mechanism and alternatives

Primary proposed mechanism: `MEC-001`. Important alternative: `MEC-002`. Other candidates include liquidity vacuum resolution, hidden order behavior, and ordinary mean reversion.

## Observable variables

- Real-time impulse-state definition.
- `SIG-001` level and decay using prespecified windows.
- `SIG-002` flow level and persistence.
- Forward executable continuation and adverse excursion at fixed or event-time horizons.

## Falsification

Reject or narrow the claim if the incremental effect disappears out of sample, flips under reasonable parameter perturbations, is unavailable before the outcome, or cannot exceed realistic spread, fees, latency, and slippage.

## Belief state

- Phenomenon exists: unmeasured.
- Predictive: unmeasured.
- Incremental: unmeasured.
- Economically exploitable: unmeasured.
