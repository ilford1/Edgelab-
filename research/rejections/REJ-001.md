# REJ-001 — Raw delta divergence as standalone reversal edge

## Rejected claim

"Price and delta diverged near a peak, therefore the divergence predicts reversal."

## Why rejected

The claim is underspecified, often uses a future-known peak, confounds activity with response, lacks an incremental baseline, and does not establish that a reversal was tradable after costs.

## What remains useful

Raw flow can remain an input to a narrower causal hypothesis such as `HYP-001`. Reopening the standalone claim requires a real-time event definition, falsifier, baseline, holdout, and execution evidence. Preserve this record even if a successor hypothesis performs better.
