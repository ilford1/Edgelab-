# MEC-002 — Mechanical volatility normalization

## Alternative explanation

The ratio called response efficiency may fall because its denominator grows, volatility changes, price discretization binds, or the selected window mixes different event states. No special passive participant behavior is required.

## Discriminating test

Compare raw and normalized components, matched volatility/spread/depth controls, alternative denominator stabilization, and event-time definitions. A genuine absorption mechanism should predict observations beyond the mathematical coupling.
