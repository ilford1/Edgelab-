# MEC-003 — Price-insensitive forced-flow dislocation

> Proposed, source-derived mechanism. It is not a measured finding or a validated trading edge.

## Participant sequence

1. A participant trades to satisfy a constraint or external objective—for example liquidation, leverage rebalancing, hedging, index flow, or urgent inventory reduction—so execution is less sensitive to price than ordinary value-seeking flow.
2. When this demand for immediacy is large relative to available and replenishing liquidity, it moves the executable price away from nearby cross-venue or relative-value anchors.
3. Other participants observe the flow directly or infer it from trades, quotes, venue lead-lag, and timing. They may front-run predictable flow, provide liquidity to it, or offset the acquired risk elsewhere.
4. Competition pulls impact earlier, smooths it, and compresses the attainable edge. After the initiating flow stops, price may revert, stabilize, or continue depending on information content, inventory, liquidity refill, and subsequent flow.

## Observable implications

- Aggressive traded size or count is unusually large relative to a causal local baseline.
- Price impact per unit flow depends on spread, depth, replenishment, and venue leadership.
- A scheduled or state-triggered event clock may improve identification when the participant constraint is knowable before the outcome.
- Cross-venue prices can lead the local contract while a stale or illiquid venue catches up.
- Any post-flow reversion or continuation must be measured separately from the initiating impact.
- Fill probability, queue position, latency, market impact, and taker costs determine whether an observed dislocation is executable.

## Cross-venue refinement

A [2026 HyperTrend Q&A](https://x.com/ScottPh77711570/status/2096760358568628681) adds a practitioner claim that intraday momentum on a local venue may improve when its reference price comes from the dominant price-discovery venue, described there as mostly Binance. The same answer warns that local catch-up can resolve too quickly for non-market-makers to monetize. Preserved in `KI-20260913-003`, this narrows the research target: measure predictive venue leadership separately from executable lag, then sweep observation delay, order latency, fill assumptions, participation, impact, and fees. The Q&A is not empirical evidence that this effect exists now.

## Boundaries and alternatives

Large aggressive flow is not proof of price insensitivity, liquidation, or uninformed trading. The same tape can reflect informed trading, news, hidden liquidity, inventory transfer, or ordinary volatility. Aggressive-flow magnitude alone does not establish temporary mispricing, and the end of forced flow does not guarantee reversal.

This mechanism is upstream of, and may interact with, `MEC-001`: passive liquidity can absorb the flow, be overwhelmed by it, or replenish after it. `IND-001` may describe an observable flow shock, but it must not assign the participant identity or mechanism without independent evidence.

## Falsifiers

- The proposed participant constraint cannot be identified using information available at decision time.
- Comparable events do not show incremental price impact after volatility, spread, depth, venue leadership, and news controls.
- The apparent effect disappears with executable prices or realistic fill assumptions.
- Post-flow behavior is indistinguishable from matched events without the proposed constraint.
- The mechanism or timing changes after competitors adapt, leaving no stable conditional effect.
