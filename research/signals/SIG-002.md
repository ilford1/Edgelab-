# SIG-002 — Aggressive trade flow

## Definition

Signed buyer-initiated minus seller-initiated traded quantity over a declared window. Store gross buy and sell quantities as well as net flow so netting does not erase intensity.

## Data requirements

Document the aggressor-classification method, timestamp source, aggregation clock, late or duplicate trade handling, and venue. If exchange flags are unavailable, quantify classification uncertainty.

## Known traps

Aggressive flow is contemporaneous activity, not inherently predictive. Delta, volume, and trade count may encode overlapping information.
