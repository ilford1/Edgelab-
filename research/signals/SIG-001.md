# SIG-001 — Price response efficiency

## Candidate definition

For a declared direction and window, measure signed price progress divided by a stabilized measure of same-side aggressive flow. Define price, direction, window, denominator floor, and normalization before testing.

## Real-time constraint

Use only trades and quotes received by the decision timestamp. Do not center windows on a future pivot or normalize with future volatility.

## Variants to compare

- Clock time versus trade-, volume-, or impulse-relative event time.
- Mid-price, microprice, or executable bid/ask progress.
- Level, slope, decay, and saturation relative to matched baselines.

Variants are candidates, not independent confirmations.

## Volume-per-move relationship

The “density” proposal captured in `KI-20260913-004` uses total volume divided by absolute price movement. It is related to, but not interchangeable with, this signal:

- `volume / (absolute price move + floor)` is an inverse response measure. A high value can indicate a thick or absorbing market, but also two-sided churn or market making.
- `absolute price move / (volume + floor)` measures displacement per unit total activity. A high value is more consistent with thinness or weak resistance.
- This node instead uses signed price progress relative to same-side aggressive flow. Total volume, signed aggressive volume, trade count, and quote updates must not be silently substituted for one another.

Price velocity (`return / clock time`) and trade-arrival intensity (`trade count / clock time`) are separate observables. A return cannot identify actual trade speed without event timestamps. Any two-period smoothing must declare the causal filter and cannot use future bars.
