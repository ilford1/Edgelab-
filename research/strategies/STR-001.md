# STR-001 — Response-decay trade-avoidance rule

> Illustrative and blocked. No trading edge or profitability is claimed.

## Concept

Suppress otherwise-qualified momentum entries while causal response efficiency is deteriorating and data quality is acceptable. Avoidance is tested against the original entry system, not against an arbitrary always-trade baseline.

## Promotion gates

Requires validated incremental evidence for `HYP-001`, stable real-time implementation of `SIG-001`, prespecified interaction with the base strategy, chronological holdout results, and realistic opportunity-cost plus execution analysis.
