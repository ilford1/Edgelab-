# EXP-001 — Matched event-time response-decay test

> Design example only. No experiment has been run.

## Target

Test `HYP-001` without defining events from future-known turning points.

## Design

Declare a causal impulse-state rule, then identify response-decay events using only data through event time. Match or weight controls on pre-event trend, impulse magnitude/age, realized volatility, spread, visible depth, venue, and time of day.

## Outcomes and baselines

Measure executable continuation, reversal, maximum favorable/adverse excursion, and time-to-event across prespecified horizons. Compare against unconditional, momentum-only, volatility-only, and raw-flow baselines.

## Validation split

Freeze definitions on development data; use chronological holdout and venue/month transfer tests. Report effect distributions and uncertainty, not only win rate.

## Leakage audit

Audit timestamps, late messages, book reconstruction, label windows, normalization windows, parameter selection, and overlapping events.

## Execution gate

Do not promote to strategy work until the signal is incremental and survives executable-price, fees, latency, slippage, and capacity assumptions.
