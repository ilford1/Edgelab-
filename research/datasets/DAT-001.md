# DAT-001 — Illustrative BTC trade and quote event stream

> Data contract only. No data is included.

## Required fields

Venue and instrument identifiers, exchange and receive timestamps, sequence identifiers when available, trade price/quantity/aggressor side, best bid/ask price and size, and reconstruction-quality flags.

## Versioning and quality

A real dataset node must state source, symbol contract, timezone, collection interval, gaps, duplicates, timestamp precision, clock synchronization, venue outages, filtering, checksum or immutable version, and licensing constraints.
