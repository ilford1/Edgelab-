# Edge Hypothesis

Route existing concepts, signals, mechanisms, rejections, and open questions before creating a new hypothesis. Extend an existing node when the idea is materially the same.

## Formulation

Separate the chain:

`observation -> proposed mechanism -> real-time signal -> conditional future outcome -> possible edge -> possible strategy`

Write the hypothesis as a conditional distribution claim with:

- population, venue/instrument, horizon, and event clock;
- information available at decision time `t`;
- measurable signal and target;
- conditioning variables and obvious baselines;
- expected direction and scope;
- null hypothesis and explicit falsifiers;
- alternative mechanisms and confounders;
- evidence required for prediction, incremental value, robustness, and economic promotion.

Prefer the simplest measure that captures the proposed phenomenon. Avoid stacking correlated confirmations. If retrospective labels such as peaks or impulses are useful, define them separately from the deployable real-time state.

Output a concise hypothesis card suitable for `research/templates/hypothesis.md`. Do not promote it to a signal, edge, indicator, or strategy without the corresponding evidence.
